YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy
Policy.

Effective date: 21 November 2026Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information Creativity handles, why it is needed, where it is stored and what choices you have. It covers the Creativity Discord bot and this informational website.

1. Who operates Creativity

Creativity is an independently operated Discord application. In this policy, “we” means the people maintaining the Creativity project. For privacy questions, use the support contact listed at the end of this policy.

2. Information we handle

When you use bot commands or interactive features, the bot may store the following information in its application database:

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID and, where a feature needs it, server (guild), channel or role IDs.
  • Account and profile details: profile handle, tagline, biography, website links, location text, status and skills that you choose to provide.
  • Feature data: Spark balances, reward claims, streaks, usage counts, settings, achievements and other progress needed to provide the bot's features.
  • Community content: teams, memberships, projects, resource listings, shop and marketplace listings, portfolio entries, event information, polls, feedback posts and related replies that you submit.
  • Connections you request: a GitHub username and verification state if you choose to link a GitHub account. The verification code is temporary and is used to check the public GitHub profile bio.
  • Uploaded files: images or other attachments you submit to features that accept them. These may be stored so the relevant post or feature continues to work.
  • Server configuration: settings selected by server administrators, such as the channel and time for Daily Spark posts.

We do not need your Discord password and do not ask you to provide it.

3. How we use information

We use this information to run commands, maintain profiles and progress, calculate rewards, display user-created content, support teams and marketplaces, apply settings, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain the service. We do not sell personal information.

4. Public and shared information

Some information is intended to be visible to other users. Examples include public profiles, team pages, project pages, public resource listings, marketplace listings and community posts. Other information may be shown only in a private or ephemeral Discord response when the feature supports it. Do not submit information to a public feature if you do not want other users to see it.

5. Storage and retention

The bot stores application data in JSON files and related folders. The project is configured to use a persistent Railway volume when deployed with the recommended /data mount. Uploaded images and feature files may be stored alongside the database.

The bot also creates periodic database snapshots and keeps up to seven snapshot files in its data folder. This means information removed from the live database may remain in a backup until that backup is rotated or deleted. We do not currently publish a fixed retention period for every type of content.

We keep information for as long as it is needed to operate the relevant feature, maintain account progress, protect the service or meet applicable obligations. Actual retention can vary by data type and operational circumstances.

6. Third-party services

Creativity depends on Discord to receive commands and deliver responses. Discord processes information under its own policies. Optional features may contact GitHub's public API or other public-data endpoints to retrieve information you request, such as a public profile, repository, image, map or news result. Those providers may receive technical request information, such as the requested public URL or query and standard network metadata.

This website is designed as a static information site. It does not include an account system, contact form, advertising pixels, analytics SDK or browser-based tracking cookies. The website is hosted on Vercel; Vercel may process ordinary request and security logs as part of hosting and delivery under its own privacy terms.

7. Security

We use the safeguards available in the project's hosting and application setup, but no online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to the bot's hosting environment and persistent storage should be restricted to authorized maintainers. Never share secrets, passwords or private credentials through public bot features.

8. Your choices and data requests

You can edit or remove some information through the relevant bot commands, such as unlinking a GitHub account or updating profile and community content. Available controls depend on the feature.

To ask about information associated with your Discord user ID, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact the project through the support server. Include your Discord user ID and a clear description of the request. We may need to verify that the request is yours. Some information may need to remain temporarily in backups or be retained where required for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

9. Children and age requirements

Creativity is intended for people who are allowed to use Discord under Discord's minimum age rules and the laws that apply to them. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information in violation of those rules. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact us so we can review the request.

10. International processing

Discord, Vercel, Railway and public-data providers may process information in countries different from yours. Their own terms and privacy policies govern the processing they perform. The exact location of infrastructure may depend on the provider's service configuration.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the bot's features, infrastructure or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through the Creativity support server.

Before you publish

Check this policy against the live bot and hosting configuration. In particular, confirm the actual database fields, file uploads, public-data providers, backup routine and data-request process. This draft is not legal advice and may need review for the operator's jurisdiction.
